The bar and the metal blocks

The two equal blocks slide along the bar. Their centers stay at -d and +d, measured from the bar's midpoint, so the center of mass of the whole system stays at the midpoint. The axis is perpendicular to the page and can move along the bar.

The masses can move from d = 1.75 m, with their outer edges at the bar's ends, to d = 0.25 m, where they meet at the midpoint. They do not overlap.

Finite block size and the bar's own inertia are included. For a uniform rectangle of mass m, width w and height h, about an axis perpendicular to the rectangle:

ICM=m12(w2+h2),I=ICM+mr2

Each distance r runs from the axis to that object's center of mass.
