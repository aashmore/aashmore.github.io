Model

The water is represented by a small mass of 1 kg at radius r. The bucket is driven at constant speed, and rotates so that its base faces away from the center.

FN is the combined normal force from the bucket's base and side walls. Away from the top and bottom, the side walls supply the tangential force that balances the tangential component of gravity.

The animation pauses when the force from the base first reaches zero below the minimum speed. Press Play again to follow the water in free fall. This part treats the water as a small drop and neglects subsequent collisions with the bucket and splashing until it reaches the floor. Real water can slosh and spill earlier.

The radial unit vector points outwards from the center. The tangential unit vector points anticlockwise. After contact is lost, these vectors remain attached to the bucket's circular path.

The arrows share a force scale within each panel. The enlarged diagram has its own scale. The bucket size is exaggerated to show the water.
